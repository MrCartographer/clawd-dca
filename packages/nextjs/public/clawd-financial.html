Why a 2×3 grid (and not a slider)

  • Comparison wins on a suite page — users scan all six products at once instead of one-at-a-time. Crucial when half are "Soon" — they signal direction even if they aren't clickable yet.
  • Sliders hide content — only ~33% of users ever interact with carousel arrows. You'd be burying 5 of 6 strategies.
  • Mobile still works — the grid collapses to 2-up at tablet, 1-up at phone. A slider is no better on small screens — it just becomes a horizontal scroll.
  • When a slider would win: if each card grew to ~480px wide with full configuration UI inline. That's a different page (one product per screen with tabs), not a suite page.

Alternative: horizontal slider

Same six products, scrollable. Try it side-by-side. Notice how only ~2.5 cards are visible at a time — you lose the bird's-eye-view advantage.
