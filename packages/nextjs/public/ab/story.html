A short fable for impatient buyers

The Patient
Crab.

There are two ways to walk into a market.
This is the story of both.

Chapter one

Once upon a market…

Prices rose and fell with the tides. Up. Down. Up again. Nobody could say which way the next wave would crash.

Chapter two — the gull

The Gull bought it all in one beak-full.

She saw the price on a Tuesday and bought everything she had. By Friday she was watching it sink. Timing the market is a bird's game, the old crabs warned. She did not listen.

Chapter three — the crab

The Crab bought a little, with every tide.

Three hours, a day, a week — it didn't matter which rhythm she chose. She just showed up. Sometimes the price was high. Sometimes low. The crab kept walking sideways into the market.

Chapter four — years pass

And then the seasons turned.

The Gull, exhausted from watching prices, had stopped looking. The Crab, who never watched, opened her shell to count what the tides had brought.
